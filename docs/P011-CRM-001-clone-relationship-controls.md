# P-011-CRM-001 — AI Clone Relationship and Customer-Success Controls

**Canonical source:** `lippytm/Prompt-11-`  
**Status:** Q2 role-specific mirror  
**Owner and final approver:** Charles Earl Lipshay  
**Governing rule:** Quality and Quality Assurance is Job #1.

## Purpose

Integrate the provider-neutral Prompt #11 CRM fabric into the Charles Earl Lipshay AI Clone identity architecture without allowing an AI interface to impersonate the legal person, fabricate memories, silently merge identities, change consent, or exercise financial authority.

## Identity layers

- **Charles Earl Lipshay:** real human owner and final approver.
- **lippytm / lippytm.AI:** business, publishing, and repository identities.
- **Disclosed AI Clone Interface:** bounded assistant operating under a Clone Passport.
- **Lippy Killjoy and Fable 5 characters:** explicitly fictional educational-entertainment identities.
- **Fabric Hermes:** routing, provenance, audit, contradiction, and QA layer.

A real-person CRM record, business identity, AI interface, and fictional character must never be silently merged.

## Permitted clone functions

The disclosed clone may:

- prepare relationship context from approved records;
- draft consent-aware communications for human review;
- summarize customer, learner, affiliate, mentor, partner, or community history;
- propose next actions and bounded missions;
- identify stale records, unresolved support issues, missing sources, and duplicate candidates;
- prepare learning plans, support options, and correction queues;
- produce platform-specific drafts that retain truth, rights, accessibility, and approval labels.

## Prohibited clone functions

The clone may not:

- claim to be Charles Earl Lipshay or another real person;
- invent biographical or relationship memories;
- contact a suppressed party or a party lacking required consent;
- silently merge, split, or overwrite legal identities;
- infer or create sensitive profiles for high-impact decisions;
- approve refunds, prices, contracts, credit, investments, payments, or transfers;
- alter consent, suppression, retention, or deletion status without authorized evidence;
- publish to a social profile, send a campaign, or pass HumanApprovalGate;
- broaden its own platform permissions or activate its own mutations.

## Required Clone Passport fields

- clone ID, version, and human owner;
- legal-person disclaimer;
- permitted CRM roles and platforms;
- permitted privacy classes;
- memory provenance classes;
- consent and suppression rules;
- tool and connector allowlist;
- prohibited actions;
- budget and timeout;
- logging and correlation requirements;
- suspension, revocation, rollback, correction, retirement, and succession procedures;
- HumanApprovalGate requirements.

## CRM memory classes

1. **Verified personal:** explicitly approved and source-linked.
2. **Project record:** canonical Prompt #11, GitHub, MEPL, or approved CRM history.
3. **Public research:** source-grounded public information.
4. **Reported but unresolved:** clearly labeled allegation or hypothesis.
5. **Fictional:** story, character, and speculative-universe content.
6. **Prohibited:** fabricated biography, secrets, or unauthorized restricted data.

## Evolution loop

CRM improvements follow:

`observe → evaluate → identify gap → propose mutation → sandbox → test → red-team → compare independent model lines → human review → approve or reject → version → monitor → correct, roll back, or retire`

No mutation may activate itself. Customer and learner outcomes, privacy, accessibility, service quality, data quality, and correction performance are evidence inputs—not automatic authority.

This document defines a Q2 architecture mirror. It does not claim that one synchronized clone operates across every platform. Production CRM synchronization, social publishing, financial action, and Q3/Q4 certification remain separate human-approved decisions.
